About

Researcher, mentor, and future academic.

My work focuses on developing physically grounded and computationally efficient approaches for challenging problems in optics and photonics. I am especially interested in research questions where analytical understanding, numerical methods, and experimentally relevant modeling must work together.

I am preparing for a long-term career in research and higher education, with particular interest in R1 faculty positions and highly competitive postdoctoral environments that enable independent scientific growth.
